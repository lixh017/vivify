# Panda Episode #001 — Storyboard (镜头分镜)

> 抖音 58s 版本,6 个镜头。每个镜头给可灵/即梦 prompt 写手可直接复制。

## 镜头 1 — 黑屏白字 (钩子) [0:00-0:03]

```
视觉: 纯黑底 + 1 行白字
文字: "凌晨 3 点,你在干什么?"
字体: 默认无衬线, 居中, 字号 = 屏幕宽度的 8%
时长: 3 秒
BGM: 此时无声 (这 3 秒的"反问"配静音反而更狠)

可灵 prompt: (静态, 不需要图生视频)
"deep black background, white sans-serif text '凌晨 3 点,你在干什么?'
centered, font size 8% of screen width, no other elements,
cinematic, 4K, vertical 9:16"
```

## 镜头 2 — 熊猫背影 (窗边) [0:03-0:08]

```
视觉: 室内, 深夜。熊猫背对镜头, 坐小凳, 窗外城市灯光远景
构图: 中远景, 熊猫占画面 1/3 左侧, 窗占右侧 2/3
灯光: 室内暖黄台灯 (低照度), 窗外冷蓝城市
时长: 5 秒
镜头运动: 慢推 (从全景推到中景, 焦距变化而非切)

可灵 prompt (图生视频):
"cinematic indoor scene, a cute giant panda wearing simple
casual home clothes, seen from behind, sitting on a small
wooden stool by a tall window. Outside the window is a soft
out-of-focus city night skyline with warm distant lights.
Indoor lighting: dim warm yellow table lamp from screen-left.
Camera: slow push-in from wide to medium, 5 seconds.
Aspect 9:16, 4K, atmospheric, contemplative."
```

## 镜头 3 — 熊猫侧脸 (端茶) [0:08-0:14]

```
视觉: 切到熊猫侧脸特写, 端起没喝完的茶
构图: 侧脸, 茶在前景 (虚焦), 窗在背景
时长: 6 秒
BGM 起: 钢琴 + 雨声白噪, 60 BPM
镜头运动: 静态

可灵 prompt:
"close-up side profile of a cute giant panda, holding a half-
full cup of tea with both paws, looking down at the tea.
Background: rain on a window, soft bokeh. Warm indoor
lighting from off-screen. Subtle steam rising from tea.
Static shot, contemplative expression, 6 seconds.
Atmospheric, melancholic, 4K, vertical 9:16."
```

## 镜头 4 — 熊猫正面 (看茶) [0:14-0:20]

```
视觉: 正面中景, 熊猫没看镜头, 看自己的茶, 表情平静但有重量
构图: 中景, 熊猫居中, 茶在手, 窗外雨
时长: 6 秒
镜头运动: 慢推到近景

可灵 prompt:
"medium shot of a cute giant panda, frontal view, looking down
at the tea in its paws, NOT looking at camera. Slightly
downcast eyes, contemplative, a quiet weight in the
expression. Background: rainy window with soft bokeh.
Warm indoor lighting. Camera: slow push-in to close-up over
6 seconds. Atmospheric, introspective, 4K, 9:16."
```

## 镜头 5 — 抬头看窗外 [0:20-0:28]

```
视觉: 熊猫抬头, 看向窗外 (不是镜头)
构图: 中近景, 镜头跟随转头, 推到窗外城市
时长: 8 秒 (这是最重要的留白)
镜头运动: 慢推到窗外远景, 焦平面从熊猫移到城市

可灵 prompt:
"medium close-up of a cute giant panda slowly lifting its
head to look out a window (away from camera, NOT at
camera). Camera: rack focus from panda's face to the
out-of-focus city skyline outside, slow push-in toward the
window. The panda's reflection becomes visible in the
window. Contemplative, 8 seconds, atmospheric, 4K, 9:16."
```

## 镜头 6 — 手机评论区 (反差) [0:28-0:36]

```
视觉: 手机屏幕特写, 抖音评论区滚动
滚动内容: "加油""太治愈了""emo 了"emoji 飘过
熊猫的手 (虚焦, 前景) 轻轻划过屏幕, 不点赞
构图: 特写, 手机占画面 80%
时长: 8 秒

可灵 prompt (or 剪映模板更稳):
"extreme close-up of a smartphone screen showing a Douyin
(Chinese TikTok) video comments section scrolling fast with
emoji and short text like '加油' '太治愈了' 'emo'. A panda's
paw hovers in the soft-focus foreground, gently sweeping
across the screen but not tapping to like any comment.
8 seconds, atmospheric, contemplative, 4K, 9:16."
```

## 镜头 7 — 站起来 + 拉镜 [0:36-0:48]

```
视觉: 熊猫把茶放下, 站起来伸个懒腰。镜头从窗内拉到窗外
构图: 全景 → 拉镜到城市 (背对熊猫)
时长: 12 秒 (这是最长的镜头, 给观众消化用)
镜头运动: 拉镜 + 慢推到窗外

可灵 prompt:
"wide shot of a cute giant panda putting down a tea cup,
standing up, and stretching with a quiet sigh. Camera:
slow pull-back from the interior through the window, the
panda becoming smaller while the calm city night skyline
becomes more visible. The city is not lonely-looking but
quiet-looking. 12 seconds, contemplative, atmospheric,
4K, 9:16."
```

## 镜头 8 — 黑屏白字 (共鸣) [0:48-0:55]

```

时长: 7 秒 (留白 + 字幕慢慢显现)

"deep black background, white sans-serif text '凌晨 3 点,
你也在,够了。' centered, slow fade-in over 7 seconds, font
size 6% of screen width, contemplative, cinematic, 4K,
vertical 9:16."
```

## 镜头 9 — 背影 + 收尾字幕 [0:55-0:58]

### 三、音频 (Suno + 剪映库)

| 元素 | 来源 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 旁白 | Suno v3.5 男声 | 30+, 慢语速, 留呼吸感, 0.85x 速 |
| 钢琴 | 剪映自带库 "深夜钢琴" | 60 BPM, 6 分钟无重复 |
| 雨声 | Pixabay 雨天白噪 30min | 全程, 末尾渐收 |
| 钩子静音 | 镜头 1-2 之间 | 0.3 秒, 制造反差 |

### 四、拍摄时间建议

- **凌晨 3-5 点** 实拍 (用真实凌晨氛围的窗外光) — 这是核心资产
- **早 7-9 点** 补拍室内 (自然光备用)
- **AI 操作手配 Suno + 剪映总工时**:约 4-6 小时 (含抽卡)
